Our Story

Trading Excellence
Since Two Decades

Aquafine Products is one of India's leading seafood traders and export representatives, headquartered at White Water's Phase 2, Thevara, Cochin — with offices extending into Oman. Our deep roots in Kerala's coastline give us unparalleled access to the finest marine harvests.

With over 15 FDA & EU approved packing units spanning all coastal regions, we ensure every shipment meets the highest international standards — from harvest to delivery.

FDA & EU Certified
All 15+ partner packers hold full international certifications for global market access.
Global Export Network
Exporting to UAE, USA, EU, China, Japan and 50+ markets worldwide.
IQF & Block Frozen
All specifications available — cooked, H/L, PUD, Blanched, Block or IQF as per client needs.
